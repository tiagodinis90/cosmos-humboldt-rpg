# COSMOS: The Journey of Alexander von Humboldt

A fully playable web-based RPG prototype that implements the core mechanics from the Humboldtian RPG design document.

## 🎮 Game Features

### Core Systems
- **Skill-Based Dialogue System**: Disco Elysium-style conversations with skill-gated options
  - Logic 🔬: Scientific reasoning, instrument use, deduction
  - Empathy 🤝: Cultural understanding, interpersonal connection
  - Aesthetics 🎨: Artistic perception, creative synthesis
  - Political Economy ⚖️: Ethical reasoning, diplomatic discourse

- **Resource Management**: Track your expedition's vital statistics
  - Health ❤️: Physical condition (game over at 0)
  - Supplies 🎒: Food, equipment, trade goods (game over at 0)
  - Morale ✨: Mental and emotional state
  - Scientific Data 📊: Accumulated observations and measurements

- **Exploration System**: Travel through 9 historically accurate locations
  - Berlin, Prussia (Starting point)
  - Caracas, Venezuela
  - The Venezuelan Llanos
  - Lake Valencia
  - The Orinoco River
  - Andes Foothills
  - Chimborazo Summit (Ecuador)
  - New Spain (Mexico)
  - Paris, France (Final synthesis)

- **Journal & Naturgemälde**: Build your scientific legacy
  - Collect observations and discoveries
  - Visualize the "Web of Life" through the Naturgemälde
  - Connect disparate phenomena into a unified understanding

### Gameplay Loop
1. **Character Creation**: Allocate skill points across 4 faculties
2. **Exploration**: Visit locations, discover new areas
3. **Dialogue**: Engage with historical figures (Bonpland, Goethe, Jefferson, Darwin)
4. **Events**: Undertake scientific challenges with skill checks
5. **Synthesis**: Build your journal and Naturgemälde
6. **Victory**: Complete Kosmos and achieve 100+ scientific data points

### Historical Content
- **15+ Unique Dialogues** with skill-gated choices
- **20+ Scientific Events** including:
  - Investigating Lake Valencia's desiccation
  - Capturing electric eels in the Orinoco
  - The ascent of Chimborazo
  - Creating the Naturgemälde
  - Writing Kosmos
- **30+ Journal Entries** documenting discoveries
- **Historically accurate locations** from Humboldt's 1799-1804 expedition

## 🎯 How to Play

1. **Start**: Click "Begin Your Journey" on the title screen
2. **Create Character**: Distribute 4 additional skill points (base: 3 in each skill)
3. **Explore**: Read location descriptions, engage in conversations, trigger events
4. **Make Choices**: Select dialogue options based on your skills and goals
5. **Manage Resources**: Keep health and supplies above 0, build morale and data
6. **Travel**: Move between locations to progress the expedition
7. **Win**: Complete the Kosmos synthesis with 100+ data points

- Dark forest green and gold color scheme evoking 19th-century scientific journals

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation

Built with:
- React + TypeScript
- Vite for fast development and building
- Tailwind CSS for styling
- Custom game state management with useReducer
- SVG-based Naturgemälde visualization

## 📚 Historical Accuracy

All locations, dialogues, and events are based on Alexander von Humboldt's actual expedition (1799-1804) and his published works. Key historical elements include:

- The partnership with Aimé Bonpland
- The investigation of Lake Valencia's desiccation (early climate change observation)
- The electric eel expedition on the Orinoco
- The record-breaking ascent of Chimborazo (19,286 feet)
- The creation of the Naturgemälde (first ecological visualization)
- Meetings with Jefferson, Goethe, and mentorship of Darwin
- The lifelong project of writing Kosmos

## 🎓 Educational Value

Players learn about:
- The scientific method and empirical observation
- Ecological interconnectedness
- The relationship between science and art
- Historical exploration and its ethical complexities
- The unity of natural knowledge
