## 🎮 Quick Start

1. **Open the game** - See the animated title screen with sailing ship
2. **Click "Set Sail"** - Begin your journey
3. **Allocate 4 skill points** - Choose your strengths
4. **Roll dice** - Watch them tumble and settle
5. **Assign to actions** - Click dice, then click actions
6. **Resolve cycle** - See results and progress
7. **Open world map** - Click mini map or 🗺️ button
8. **Travel** - Click locations to journey there
9. **Experience weather** - See rain, snow, fireflies per location
10. **Complete Kosmos** - Win the game!
